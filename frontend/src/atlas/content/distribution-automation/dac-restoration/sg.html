Settings groups

§15DA Lib / DA Adapter 105.0.1.100Code-derived
  • Each SG-capable device (breakers, reclosers, pseudo breakers) has up to six source→group pairs. The device must be in the group mapped to whichever source currently feeds it (actual topology). Equipment/SettingGroup/EQUIPMENT_SETTING_GROUP_CHECK
  • IncorrectSG (device, feeder and scheme outputs) asserts after SgAlarmDelay (0 s) when the device is in the wrong group, but not within 10 s of a feeder event, RTN or an active SG command.
  • During restoration the DAC changes groups before closing the tie (§14). During RTN it puts them back; RTN completion requires NormalSG (normally-closed devices on the feeder correct for the normal source).
  • A device with InhibitControl is skipped for SG changes.
  • Adapter: SettingsGroupControlType (e.g. SINGLE_PULSE) controls how the SSn output is delivered; SG1 status can be inverted by AlternateSettings.
