DAC — Alarms & SCADA Points

Every DAC-generated status and control, what asserts it, and what a combination of them means

DAC_ · Feeder_ · Device_SPS · MV · DNPCwhy it failed

☰How mapping works

  • Points are claimed by naming tags in the SCADA DNP server map to match a hard-coded template. The map is named on the DAC Extension Settings tab.
  • Every status and control is optional. Name the tag to get the point.
  • Three prefixes: DAC_ for system-wide, Feeder_<BreakerName>_ for per-feeder, Device_<DeviceName>_ for per-device.
  • Physical breakers, reclosers and switches support the same device-level set. Virtual breakers do not.
  • After changing tag names in the SCADA map without touching extension settings, set Re-generate Configuration = True to force a re-parse.

Tag types: SPS single-point status · MV measured value · DNPC DNP control.

DAC_ReconfigureStep / DAC_ReconfigureStepTimeout SPSReconfiguration in progress / exceeded ReconfigurationTimeLimit.

Overload and shed

Why it failed — the four

PointMeaning
DAC_CapacityLimit SPSRestoration failed due to a capacity limit. No specific cause is given. For RTN it also covers voltage, transition type and load-break-incapable NO points.
DAC_AbnormalCondition SPSRestoration failed due to an abnormal condition.
DAC_VoltageLimit SPSRestoration failed due to a voltage limit.
DAC_CommandFailure SPSRestoration failed due to a command failure.

Controls

PointOperation
DAC_EnableDisableCommand DNPCoperClose enables, operTrip disables.
DAC_EnableCommand / DAC_DisableCommand DNPCSeparate points for the same two actions.

_ReconfigurationStartedFeeder has started reconfiguration.

_CapacityLimitFailed restoration due to a capacity limit.
