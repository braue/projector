Worked examples with timelines

Three events followed second by second: what the field does, what the DAC does in response, and the log entries you'd expect to see.

§27DA Lib / DA Adapter 105.0.2Code-derived
How to read these Times are illustrative and use Adapter defaults. They assume devices answer polls in 1–2 s and report status changes within 2–3 s. The order of events and the gating conditions come from the code. The exact seconds depend on your communications and protection settings. Log entries are shown as Name: Description with Asserted/Deasserted, which is how the Adapter writes them (§28).

If LossOfSourceRestoreTime is set above 0, at t = 70 the feeder raises LossOfSource and the LossOfSourceRestoreWindow analog counts down. Nothing switches unless an operator sends LossOfSourceRestoreCmd inside the window. This is also why the Adapter raises the configuration alarm Non-Zero LossOfSourceRestoreTime Set. If the source returns before confirmation, the open phase clears and nothing happens.

A 0.1 A connector margin means the DAC acted correctly on what it was told. The question is why the remote side offered no margin: a dead side, its permissive, a clamp to the dead side's margin, or a stale connector watchdog. This is the pattern seen in the Evergreen 10-19-26 review.
