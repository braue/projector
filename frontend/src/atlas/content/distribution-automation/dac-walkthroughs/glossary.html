Glossary

Terms as the libraries use them, with links to where each is explained in full.

§30DA Lib / DA Adapter 105.0.1.100Code-derived
TermMeaning
Abnormal (device)A device the DAC won't trust or operate: comm failure, remote off, hot-line tag, reclose blocked, close-on-fault, discrepancy and so on. §06
Abnormal (feeder)Any equipment in the feeder's actual topology is abnormal, or the feeder is in a confirmed loop. §02
AbnormalConditionCompletion flag: the sequence ended because of an abnormal device, a missing source, a close onto fault or a non-responding device. §03
Abnormal restoration modePer-feeder setting (None / Isolate / RestoreUpstream / RestoreAround) that lets an abnormal feeder still isolate or restore. §06
Actual topologyThe network as it is now, built from present switch states and filtered continuity. Frozen while an event is worked. §03
AdapterThe DA Adapter library: device blocks that turn relay points into DAC inputs and handle SCADA, logging, email and redundancy. §01
Alternate (source / feeder)A feeder that can pick up restored load. Must be armed and available. §02
ANALYZEFeeder step where the event is confirmed and located. §03
ARAutomatic Reconfiguration: fault isolation and service restoration, as opposed to DFO. §01
AR_EnabledEnable AND active DAC AND licensed AND no settings error. §02
ArmedFeeder state in which it detects events: AR enabled and the feeder not excluded. §02
AvailableArmed and (not abnormal, or an abnormal restoration mode is set). Only available feeders can be alternates. §02
BestSolutionThe plan the solver chose, which execution works through. §13
Block closeOptional commands that block or enable a device's own close while the DAC works. §18
CapacityLimitCompletion flag: the plan sheds load, leaves an overload, or couldn't restore everything for capacity reasons. §03
Close onto faultA DAC close followed within 10 s by a fault or trip. Latched; blocks further closes on that device. §07
Closed transitionMake-before-break transfer between sources of the same source group. Always on in this version. §14
CollaborationThe joint update/solve/execute handshake between two DACs across a connector. §17
Confirmed (event)The event passed its confirmation test in ANALYZE, for example a fault with lockout. Starts reconfiguration. §03
Connector (DA_CONNECTOR / DA_DAC_INTERCONNECT)Link to another DAC at a boundary switch. Models the far side as a source with an offered margin. §17
ContinuityWhether current can pass through a piece of equipment in the model (closed, not out of service). §06
DA_InProgressEvent or RTN detected or confirmed, analysing, reconfiguring, or collaborating for 2 s or more. Many behaviours key off it. §03
Dead / Indeterminate / Live1 / Live2Voltage levels: below 20 %, between, above 90 %, above 95 % of nominal (defaults). §05
Device definitionShared point map and behaviour settings for a type of device (indexes, normal states, scaling). §21, §29
DFO / VVODynamic Feeder Optimisation: volt/var control with capacitors and regulators. Separately licensed. §18
Event reportFile written by the Lib after each sequence: candidates, rejections, operations. §18
ExcludedFeeder taken out of automation by an operator or logic. Unarmed. §02
Extended isolationIsolation that grows past a non-controllable device, or into an adjacent bus or line zone. §07
Feeder (in the model)Everything reachable from one source, one per DA_CIRCUIT, plus one per connector. Counts toward the license. §02
Head / TailWhich DAC sources the boundary at a connector: Head = local, Tail = remote. §17
Heartbeat (SCADA)Periodic SCADA control. If it stops for HeartbeatTimeoutTime, the DAC disables itself. §04
Immediate isolationOpening the switches that bound a confirmed fault, done at confirmation before the solve. §07
Integrity pollBackground poll of every device every IntegrityPeriod (300 s), and after enable or a finished sequence. §04
Isolation pointA controllable switch at the edge of a fault zone. Flagged SW.Isolation. §07
LockoutReclosing finished with the device open. Required to confirm a permanent fault. §07
Loss of source (LOS)Open phase whose dead section starts at the source, above the first switch. §08
ManagerDA_MANAGER_AR: the scheme-level block that runs the solve and execution. §03
MapOutputsGoes TRUE 5 s after a reset. No outputs are written before then. §02
MarginSpare capacity: rating × multiplier minus present or projected load, in amps. §13
MiscoordinationAn upstream device locked out although a downstream device also saw the fault. §07
MOTThe RTAC's part number string, read for licensing. §02
NodeA connection point between equipment terminals in the model. §01
Normal topologyThe network with every device in its normal state. Used for RTN. §12
Open phaseA dead section directly below a live device, with no fault. §08
PermissivePer-feeder input that must be TRUE before the DAC puts load on it. If it's FALSE, the DAC sends RequestPermissive and waits. §14
Pseudo breakerA source breaker modelled without full measurements. §18
Reconfiguration Started / CompleteFeeder flags for a sequence that began / finished successfully. Complete self-clears after 600 s. §03
Remote eventAn event on a feeder shared with another DAC, reported through a connector. §11
RestorationFailCompletion flag: the sequence aborted, timed out or couldn't restore. Self-clears after 600 s. §03
ReturnCloseA locked-out device the plan recloses, for example after a miscoordination correction. §07
RootA de-energised area beyond an isolation point that the solver tries to restore. §13
RTNReturn to normal: putting the network back to its normal topology after repair. §12
ScanOne execution of the RTAC task. Almost everything is re-evaluated every scan. §00
SchemeOne automation domain: a DA_SCHEME with its feeders. Up to 2 per RTAC. §01
Settings errorA blocking configuration problem that disables AR. §28
Settings group (SG)Relay protection group. The DAC sets the group for the source that will feed the device before closing. §15
Source eventAn event at or behind the feeder source (bus, transformer, breaker failure), raised by logic or voltage. §09
Source groupSources that may be paralleled. Closed transition is only allowed within a group. §14
TransferDepthHow many ties away an alternate source may be. 0 blocks crossing any tie (caveat). §13
TripNewThe moment a latched fault coincides with an open or locked-out device. The fault event trigger. §07
Unresolved faultA located fault with no upstream lockout yet. Keeps the feeder in ANALYZE. §07
UPDATEFeeder step that collects fresh data from every relevant device. §03
Voltage discrepancyMeasured voltage disagrees with what the model infers. §05
WatchdogCountCounter point from each relay. It must change for a poll response to count as fresh. §04
WDOGThe DAC's periodic 5 s pulse to each device (90 s period). §04
