Remote event

A DA_CONNECTOR sets RemoteEvent.Detected on a local feeder when the DAC on the other side reports an event on the shared circuit (after the connector has been head or tail for 10 s). It confirms when the remote DAC confirms. Confirmation makes the connector's modelled source a root and flags the whole feeder for restoration, copying the remote side's shed permissive or source-group matching. Feeder/Event/Remote/class_RemoteEvent OBJ/DA_CONNECTOR

Bus restoration (substation)

DA_BUS_RESTORE handles a substation bus transfer using DA_MAIN and DA_TIE: a LossOfSource trigger supervised by TriggerSupervision, detected after DetectionTime (2 s) and confirmed after ConfirmationTime (2 s); continues if RestorationPermissive; optional fast bus restore when the bus load is below the alternate's capacity; MaxTransferableLoad; optional automatic return after the LOS has been gone for AutomaticReturnTime (10 min) with ReturnPermissive; ReturnFail latches. A confirmed bus restore makes each affected feeder's source a root with shed permissives and blocks open-phase detection while it runs.
