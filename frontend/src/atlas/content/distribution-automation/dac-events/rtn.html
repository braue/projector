Return to normal

Putting the system back in its normal configuration after a reconfiguration, preferably without another outage.

§12DA Lib / DA Adapter 105.0.2Code-derived

The rules, stated plainly

  1. RTN is requested by a rising edge of the circuit's ReturnNormal, the scheme's ReturnNormal, or a ReturnNormal on any feeder that normally feeds part of this one.
  2. It is silently ignored if an event is in progress, the feeder is unarmed, anything touched by its normal topology is abnormal or unarmed or in a temporary loop, or ReturnMode is NO_RETURN.
  3. Every device on the normal path must be either already in its normal position or operable. One unoperable off-normal device or one abnormal device aborts the whole RTN.
  4. CLOSED_TRANSITION (default) means normally-closed points are closed while still tied (make before break), which requires the same source group on both sides.
  5. A new event during RTN aborts the RTN.
  6. Non-restorable flags left by the event do not block RTN. They are cleared when the DA sequence finishes, and a close next to a returning feeder ignores NonRestorable-Branch when tracing its source. OBJ/DA_MANAGER_AR Common/GetSource/class_ExecuteGetSource

ReturnMode

ModeBehaviour
NO_RETURNRTN requests are ignored.
SG_ONLYOnly accepted when the feeder is already in its normal switching configuration; used to put settings groups back. Otherwise AbnormalCondition.
CLOSED_TRANSITION (default)Normally-closed open switches are closed first where both sides are sourced from the same source group, then the temporary tie opens. Source-group mismatch aborts (flagged CapacityLimit).
ALLOW_OPEN_TRANSITIONSource-group checks bypassed; a momentary outage (open then close) is allowed.

How the switching list is built

In ANALYZE, after polling the feeder, its adjacent feeders and every feeder touching its normal topology, mFEEDER_FindReturnSwitches walks the normal topology from the source: Feeder/Event/Return/mFEEDER_FindReturnSwitches

The resulting lists go through the same solver and execution engine as a restoration. Insufficient capacity in the solution aborts the RTN (CapacityLimit). After execution the RTN completes when the feeder reports NormalConfig and NormalSG (all normally-closed devices in the correct settings group for the normal source).

  • On RTN detection the feeder's targets and the latched flags of its equipment are reset (including command failures), and the targets of the other feeders involved are reset.
  • During RTN, solver searches only use switches that touch a feeder with RTN latched.
  • Phase-discrepancy devices get special handling: a normally-open point energised through a phase-discrepancy device is treated as connected (to avoid blinking load), and closes are only issued when one side is live and the other ≤ Live1, or the device is in phase discrepancy.
Code caveat An RTN aborted because the source groups differ sets CapacityLimit, not a source-group-specific flag. If an RTN fails with CapacityLimit on a lightly loaded system, check the SourceGroup of the circuits involved.
