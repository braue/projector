Code caveat fb_Heartbeat checks the timeout setpoint with 1 min <= setpoint OR setpoint <= 60 min. Because it uses OR, every value passes and the intended clamp to a 10-minute default never happens. A 0 s or very small setting is used as-is.

Device communication health

The rules, stated plainly

  • CommunicationFailure = comm present AND (offline > IntegrityPeriod+10 s OR not updated > IntegrityPeriod+10 s OR UpdateFailed OR never polled successfully OR fewer than 3 successful responses since the last failure).
  • A device recovering from a failure is re-polled every 10 s (COM_RECOVERY_SUBSEQUENT_POLL_DELAY) until it has 3 good responses.
  • Comm failure makes the device abnormal, forces its voltage confidence to 0 (its PTs read as Dead), disarms its open-phase detection and its PTs.
  • Devices with OnScan FALSE are not polled during an event and are ignored by the "all devices updated" checks.

In the Adapter, DeviceOnline = RTAC client online AND the device's CommStatus point (with Normal_CommStatus polarity). ADP/DA_REC.prv_StatusInputs

Polling

  • Integrity poll: every IntegrityPeriod (Adapter 5 min, Lib 300 s) while no event or RTN is detected, plus on DA enable and when a DA sequence finishes; every comm device gets a poll request. OBJ/DA_CONTROLLER
  • Event poll: on a new event the event feeder's devices are polled, plus feeders within TransferDepth hops that are armed and not abnormal. An RTN also polls every feeder touched by the normal topology. A confirmed lockout triggers one more poll of the feeder; RepollAfterLockout adds another poll of feeder and adjacent feeders.
  • DA_COM_LINE (one per port) queues poll requests and runs one at a time per port. Timeout PollTimeout (30 s), shortened to 10 s while DA is in progress. Offline devices are dropped from the queue. ConcurrentFirstAttempt issues the first attempt immediately; connectors are always polled immediately. A timed-out poll increments CommunicationFailureCount. OBJ/DA_COM_LINE
  • The POLL output is a pulse; the device watchdog pulse is held off while POLL is active.

Command delivery

Every control (open, close, settings-group change, source reversal, fault reset, backup enable/disable, permissive request) goes through a CMD block: Communications/Command/CMD

  • Pulse width CMD_PULSE = 5 s. Retries: CMD_RETRIES = 2 (three pulses total), spaced by CMD_RETRY_DELAY = 7 s, while feedback has not reached the commanded state.
  • Failure after CMD_TIMEOUT, which equals UpdateTimeLimit (Adapter 120 s, Lib 60 s). Failure sets Com.CommandFailure: the device becomes abnormal, its CMD_TIMEOUT alarm asserts, and every pending command on it is dropped.
  • A command that reaches its feedback state ends immediately (Close drops when the device reports closed, etc.).
  • OPEN/CLOSE are only written when AR is enabled, outputs are mapped, and the device is not inhibited or out of service. CLOSE is additionally blocked while the device is flagged close-on-fault or possible close-on-fault.
  • The fault-target RESET output is written even when control is inhibited, but only while the relay's fault input is still asserted.
