Voltage: levels, inference and discrepancy

Voltage decides open-phase detection, which sources are usable, and whether a close may be issued. It is evaluated per device terminal, then propagated to nodes.

Levels and thresholds

Each terminal has a level: Dead (0), Indeterminate (1), Live1 (2), Live2 (3). Thresholds come from DA_SCHEME as a percent of nominal, applied to both primary (NominalVoltage, line-to-neutral) and a 120 V secondary base, with hysteresis of VoltageDeadband: OBJ/DA_SCHEME Equipment/Measurements/Voltage/Sensor/EQUIPMENT_VOLTAGE_SENSOR

  • Analog voltage: Level uses the lowest phase; HighestPhaseLevel uses the highest phase (used for phase-discrepancy and RTN cases). Values are clamped to 0–2 × nominal.
  • Binary voltage (Dead/Live1/Live2 bits from the relay): Live and Dead both set = Indeterminate.
  • Adapter: an asserted 59N on a side forces that side to 0 V / Dead.
  • A terminal with no sensing on a closed device inherits the other side's level (INFERRED).

Confidence and node voltage

Each terminal carries a confidence: sensed 1.0, inferred through a closed device 0.95, propagated from downstream 0.75, propagated from upstream 0.5 (lower still when passing through a phase-discrepancy device, so any other reading wins). A device in comm failure or out of service gets confidence 0 and both terminals forced Dead. fb_VoltageUpdate walks from the sources and gives every node the most confident reading available, then pushes readings into unmetered branches. Voltage discrepancy

On a closed device that is not in phase discrepancy, DiscrepancyDetect sets when:

  • both sides are sensed and one is Dead while the other is ≥ Live1; or
  • sources exist in both directions and a sensed side reads Dead; or
  • the device's own side is source-facing, it reads Dead, and the downstream reading is ≥ Live1.

Held for UpdateTimeLimit it becomes DiscrepancyAlarm (a new alarm is only raised while no DA sequence is in progress). The alarm makes the device abnormal. A discrepancy device also blocks "dead" declarations through it, so it cannot be the location of an open phase. Enabled by EnableVoltageDiscrepancy (Adapter default FALSE, Lib default TRUE). Node/Voltage/fb_VoltageUpdate.prv_CheckDiscrepancy Equipment/EQUIPMENT

Interpretation Discrepancy is how the DAC catches a failed PT or bad wiring: a closed switch cannot really be dead on one side and live on the other. Making the device abnormal stops the DAC from trusting that location for open-phase or restoration decisions.
