Redundancy, SCADA control and setpoint retention

§19DA Lib / DA Adapter 105.0.1.100Code-derived
  • Up to four DACs (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, Quaternary) linked over UDP port 59000. Only the active DAC runs AR (Enable AND DacActive) and sends DA_ENABLED_WDOG. ADP/Device Function Blocks/AR/DA_Redundancy
  • Active-DAC selection, in order of what is configured: SCADA force/unforce commands; RTU voting (each RTU votes; the DAC that sees the most live RTUs wins; ties go to the higher-priority DAC, so the primary takes back over when equal; a DAC with a failed watchdog cannot win); SCADA heartbeat (the DAC that last received the heartbeat is active); otherwise manual or dual-hot failover on link loss. Transfers are delayed and conflicts resolved so only one DAC is active. ADP/Device Function Blocks/RtuVotingScheme/*
  • A SettingsVersion check between DACs prevents an out-of-date DAC taking over (bypassed when SCADA voting is in use).
  • A DA Reset command marks the DAC online.
  • Redundant point management: operator-changeable values (scheme and feature enables, heartbeat monitoring, forced DAC, feeder include/exclude, SCADA inhibit/bypass/OOS, capacities, priorities, timers listed in enumAnalogPointCodes) are replicated to the partner and saved to a setpoint file, so a reboot or failover keeps the operator's state. On first initialisation they take the setting defaults.
