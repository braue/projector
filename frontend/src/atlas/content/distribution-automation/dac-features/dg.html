DA_DG_UNIT (Adapter DA_DG) models a DG point of interconnection the DAC can trip and permit to reconnect.

§16DA Lib / DA Adapter 105.0.1.100Code-derived

The rules, stated plainly

  1. DG is tripped 5 s after its feeder has a fault, it becomes islanded, it is paralleled across a valid and an invalid circuit, or it ends up on a circuit it is not allowed on, unless its RideThrough setting covers that case. A disallowed circuit always trips.
  2. DG is given a close permissive 5 s after it is back on an allowed circuit with no fault and no DA sequence in progress.
  3. If DG exists anywhere in the system, post-outage reporting is turned off, and DG output is ignored in capacity margins.
  • Circuit[1..4]: the source (circuit) IDs the DG may be connected to. All zero = any circuit.
  • RideThrough bits: Fault (1), Isolation (2), CloseTransition (4), All (7), None (0, default).
  • Controls only act when AR is enabled, the DG is in service and not abnormal, and it has been seen connected to a source at least once since boot (and its feeder is not excluded). Trips need OpenControlAvailable; close permissive needs EnableCloseControlAvailable and is only sent while ClosePermissive feedback is FALSE.
  • The 5 s flicker delay drops to 0 when the DG is fed through a DAC connector (held 5 s).
  • Outputs: Open, EnableClose, Islanded, DisallowedCircuit. The open in progress is reported to the execution engine (LogicOpenInProgress).
  • DA_DG_CURTAILMENT (trip DG when POI export falls below a threshold) is marked obsolete in this build: "Needs updates before deployment".
