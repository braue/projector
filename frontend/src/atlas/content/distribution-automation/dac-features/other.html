Other features and device types

§18DA Lib / DA Adapter 105.0.1.100Code-derived

Block close

For devices with a CloseBlocked feedback, the DAC pulses BlockClose (1 s pulses, 10 s period) until the IED confirms the block, and EnableClose to remove it. BlockCloseMode: BlockCloseDisabled (Adapter default); AllClosedTransition (Lib default) blocks closing of an open device with sources on both sides; DifferentSourceGroup blocks only when the two sides have different source groups. A device the DAC has selected to close is unblocked. Only active while the device is online and healthy. Equipment/BlockCloseLogic/fb_BlockClose

Interpretation Block close protects against someone (or a local auto-close scheme) paralleling two live sources at a tie the DAC did not intend to close.

Underfrequency load shed support

A device's UnderFrequencyDetected (Adapter: FrequencyLockout point) is held for AnalyzeTimeLimit. It latches if the device opened within 10 s or the voltage downstream went dead for EventDetectTime. While latched, plus MAX(OpenPhaseConfirmTime, FaultHoldTime), the feeder is ExcludedByUnderFrequency (unarmed), so the DAC does not try to restore load that UFLS deliberately dropped. Scheme or feeder reset clears it. Pseudo breakers have no UF input. Feeder/UnderFrequenceTrip/fb_UF_Check Feeder/FEEDER

Non-load-break switches

  • A closed (or phase-discrepant) switch with LoadBreak FALSE that is connected to any source is automatically InhibitControl: it cannot be operated while energised. Equipment/EQUIPMENT
  • It is opened only when disconnected from every source; plans that would open it energised are rejected (BadLoadBreak).
  • Upstream of an open phase it is handled with TripReclose of a load-break device further upstream (§08).

Devices without fault detection or protection

DA_SWITCH_AV_BV has optional fault detection and no protection. A fault seen by such a switch is located normally; when an upstream protective device locks out, it is reclosed after the switch isolates, regardless of the miscoordination setting (§07). A switch with no fault detection is transparent to fault location: the fault zone extends past it.

Pseudo breakers

DA_PS_BREAKER_AV_BV (Adapter DA_PSEUDO_BKR, DA_FDR_PSEUDO_BKR) represents a breaker whose data the DAC does not have directly: protection, fault, reclose and settings groups exist, but no current measurement and no UF detection. A locked-out, inhibited pseudo breaker at the first level with no current or voltage measurement causes the breaker zone to be isolated.

Spare breaker

DA_BREAKER_AV_BV.pSpareBreaker + SpareInService: when the spare is in service the breaker's B-side connection is swapped to the spare's, both devices are cross-referenced (ActiveSwappedDevice), and a topology reset rebuilds the model. Not processed while DA is in progress.

Single-phase operate devices

In the Adapter, per-phase status and lockout points feed a single DAC status: in three-phase (gang) mode any phase not confirmed open is treated as closed, and lockout needs all phases; in single-phase lockout mode the device is "prefer open" (any phase lockout counts) except during RTN of a normally-open device. Mixed phases set PhaseDiscrepancy, which the engine handles specially in voltage inference, RTN and execution. ADP/DA_REC.prv_StatusInputs

Automatic transfer switch

DA_TRANSFER_SWITCH_AV has two ways (A/B) with their own normal and actual status, Automatic mode, and AUTO/MAN commands. No fault or current detection; load-break.

Backup groups

DA_MANAGER_BACKUP.Enable sets g_BackupEnabled, which allows EN_BACKUP/DIS_BACKUP commands to devices carrying a BackupGroup.

DFO / VVO (optional)

DA_MANAGER_OP, DA_CAPACITOR_OP, DA_REGULATOR_OP and the PermitOp/VQ_Enable pins on circuits implement voltage/var optimisation (setpoints, PF limits, critical and control excursions, daily operation limits). It is licensed separately and is independent of AR; a circuit with PermitOp cannot PermitLoop. Not covered in depth here.

Reports and logs

  • Event report (HTML) per DA event: event types (PermanentFault, Miscoordination, OpenPhase, Overload, SourceEvent, LossOfSource, ReturnToNormal, BusTransfer, Remote), chosen solution, miscoordination switches, extended-fault-zone loads, open-phase-inhibited switches, execution flags, end issues (CapacityLimit, CommandFail, VoltageLimit, WaitingForSource, CloseOnFault). Reports/EventReport/fb_DAEventRecorder
  • Execution log and mid-event log: snapshots of equipment, nodes, feeders and the best solution at the start of execution; written at completion or abort.
  • Solution logs (checked and compared states with reasons) when a writer is linked.
  • Post-outage report (customer minutes, kWh): default ON only with ≤ 10 feeders and customer counts loaded; minimum outage OutageMinimumReportingTime (5 min); tracking ends after OutageTimeoutTime (7 d) or OutageManualReset. Forced off when DG exists.
  • Adapter: SOE logging for every device point, pre-event reports from live data, settings audit, commissioning HMI, email alarms (inhibited while DA is disabled or the feeder is excluded).

Simulation mode (Adapter)

DA_SimulationMode lets device points be toggled from the HMI instead of the field (status, lockout, fault, HLT, remote, online, etc., with watchdog counters kept incrementing). It switches itself off after DA_Simulation_On_Time; leaving it disables the DAC (held off 5 s in FieldLatch_NoScada). Devices in simulation report CommissioningMode, which removes their current and power measurements from the model.
