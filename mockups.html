Screen 04

Inspect — read-only settings for one file

Two ways in: click a device box on the canvas (jumps here with that device selected), or open the Inspect tab and pick from the sidebar. Either way it's read-only browsing: full object tree on the left, settings on the right with one tab per settings page — the rtac-explorer preview, restyled. A Browse / Aggregate toggle sits in the pane: Aggregate is the settings-comparison table across objects within this file.
